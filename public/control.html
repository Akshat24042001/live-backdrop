Languages

Speech recognition

Lower is faster but may cut ideas. 400–500 is a good balance.
Browser speech free, no key
Built into Chrome and Edge (sends audio to Google). One language at a time, live word-by-word. Hindi speech in English mode comes out in Roman letters.
Groq Whisper free tier: about 2 hours of audio per hour
OpenAI's Whisper model on Groq's servers. Very accurate for English, Hindi and Gujarati. Text arrives phrase by phrase (1–2 s after each pause). Not the same company as Grok/xAI.
Sarvam (Saaras) free credits on signup, then about ₹1.5/min
Made in India for Indian languages. Best at Hindi + English or Gujarati + English mixed in one sentence ("codemix" writes English words in English letters and Hindi in Devanagari).
Soniox paid · about ₹10 per hour
Live word-by-word with Hindi, Gujarati and English mixed in one sentence. The only engine fast enough for the "about one second" goal.
